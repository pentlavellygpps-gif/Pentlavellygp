Welcome to the Official Website of Pentlavelly Grama Panchayat.
Pentlavelly Grama Panchayat is committed to providing transparent, accountable, and citizen-friendly governance. The Panchayat works continuously for village development, sanitation, drinking water supply, street lighting, road maintenance, public health, and the effective implementation of government welfare schemes.
This website has been created to provide citizens with easy access to important information, including public notices, development works, government schemes, public services, meeting updates, and other official announcements.
We encourage the active participation of citizens through their suggestions, feedback, and grievances to build a cleaner, stronger, and more developed village.
"Our Village – Our Responsibility – Our Development."
