హోమ్ గ్రామం గురించిPentlavelly Gram Panchayat, located in Pentlavelly Mandal, Nagarkurnool District, Telangana, is committed to building a clean, green, healthy, and inclusive village. With a population of approximately 8,484, the Gram Panchayat focuses on sustainable rural development through active community participation and transparent governance. The Panchayat prepares and implements the Gram Panchayat Development Plan (GPDP) by involving citizens, Women's Sabhas, Bala Sabhas, Self-Help Groups (SHGs), and line departments. Priority is given to sanitation, solid waste management, safe drinking water, internal roads, drainage, street lighting, education, healthcare, environmental protection, women's empowerment, and livelihood promotion.
